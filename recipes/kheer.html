Kheer

Description

A sweet, creamy rice pudding flavored with cardamom, cooked slowly with milk and sugar.

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Milk
  • Sugar
  • Cardamom powder
  • Almonds
  • Pistachios
  • Raisins (optional)

Steps

  1. Boil milk and add soaked rice, cook on low flame until rice is soft.
  2. Add sugar and cardamom, stir until thick and creamy.
  3. Garnish with almonds and pistachios before serving.
