Chicken Karahi

Description

A spicy and tangy Pakistani dish made with chicken, tomatoes, ginger, garlic, and fresh spices cooked in a wok (karahi).

Ingredients

  • Chicken pieces
  • Tomatoes
  • Onions
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Green chilies
  • Oil
  • Red chili powder
  • Salt
  • Fresh coriander

Steps

  1. Heat oil, sauté onions, add chicken and ginger-garlic paste, cook until slightly browned.
  2. Add tomatoes, spices, and green chilies, cook until thick gravy forms.
  3. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot.
