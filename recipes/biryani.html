Biryani

Description

A flavorful and aromatic South Asian rice dish made with spiced meat, basmati rice, and herbs, layered and cooked to perfection

Ingredients

  • Basmati rice
  • chicken or mutton
  • onions
  • tomatoes
  • yogurt
  • ginger-garlic paste
  • biryani misala
  • oil
  • fresh herbs

Steps

  1. Cook spiced meat and half-cooked rice separately
  2. then layer them in a pot with fried onions and herbs
  3. Cover and steam (dum) until rice is fully cooked and flavors blend
